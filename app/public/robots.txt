# Open to search engines since #147; until then this file said Disallow: / to all of them. The
# lines below keep out only what is no page for people: the AJAX of the comments and the video
# flag, logging out and the admin's pages. $ ends a path, so a song or an artist whose page
# starts with "comments" stays in.
#
# The crawlers of AI search, and the fetches a person asks an assistant for, are named, so each
# finds itself here and reads the same rules as everyone (#217): OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot and
# ChatGPT-User, Anthropic's Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User, Perplexity's PerplexityBot and
# Perplexity-User. So are the crawlers that gather text to train models, GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
# Google-Extended and CCBot: they are let in by decision, as a model that knows hhbd.pl can
# answer with it. Keeping one of them out takes a group of its own with Disallow: /, since a
# crawler named in a group reads that group alone.
User-agent: *
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
User-agent: ChatGPT-User
User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
User-agent: Claude-User
User-agent: PerplexityBot
User-agent: Perplexity-User
User-agent: GPTBot
User-agent: ClaudeBot
User-agent: Google-Extended
User-agent: CCBot
Disallow: /comments$
Disallow: /comments/
Disallow: /api/
Disallow: /admin/
Disallow: /admin-interface/
Disallow: /user/logout
