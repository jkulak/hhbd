# Open to search engines since #147; until then this file said Disallow: / to all of them. The
# lines below keep out only what is no page for people: the AJAX of the comments and the video
# flag, logging out and the admin's pages. $ ends a path, so a song or an artist whose page
# starts with "comments" stays in.
#
# They also keep crawlers off what is no page of its own (#286), where a week of the edge's log
# had most of ClaudeBot's and Meta's fetches: the account pages, which every page's header links
# with its own address in ?url=; the old ?autoplay= and ?showCommentForm= links, the same page at
# another address, which are fragments now; and search's tabs and pages. A search itself,
# /szukaj.html?q=<words>, stays open, as llms.txt hands it to assistants.
#
# The crawlers of AI search, and the fetches a person asks an assistant for, are named, so each
# finds itself here and reads the same rules as everyone (#217): OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot and
# ChatGPT-User, Anthropic's Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User, Perplexity's PerplexityBot and
# Perplexity-User. So are the crawlers that gather text to train models, GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
# Google-Extended, CCBot, Apple's Applebot-Extended, Meta's meta-externalagent and Amazonbot:
# they are let in by decision, as a model that knows hhbd.pl can answer with it. Keeping one of
# them out takes a group of its own with Disallow: /, since a crawler named in a group reads that
# group alone.
